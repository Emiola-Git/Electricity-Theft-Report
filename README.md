# Energy-Theft-Report

One of the biggest challenges in the energy sector is not just distribution, but the issues of energy theft and wastage.

This report provides insights into:

The percentage of customers involved in energy theft

The volume of energy lost

The estimated financial loss

By highlighting these areas, the report helps electricity stakeholders identify the root causes of losses and take action to recover revenue that would otherwise be lost.

Addressing theft and wastage is not only about revenue protection but also about building a more sustainable and reliable power system.
